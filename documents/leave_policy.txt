========================================================================
ENTERPRISE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: TIME OFF AND LEAVE POLICIES
========================================================================

1.0 ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
The organization recognizes the importance of work-life balance and provides paid annual leave to support employee well-being. All standard full-time corporate employees receive exactly 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a prorated amount determined by their contracted weekly working hours. Vacation accrual begins on the first day of employment, but days cannot be utilized until the initial onboarding baseline is completed.

2.0 SCHEDULING AND APPROVAL PROCESS
All planned annual leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR portal at least two weeks prior to the requested start date. Managers review requests based on team capacity and operational requirements. Peak business seasons may require black-out dates where annual leave is restricted.

3.0 CARRY FORWARD AND VACATION EXPIRATION
Employees are encouraged to utilize their full vacation allocation within the current calendar year. However, operational demands may prevent this. Under standard corporate policy, employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Any additional accrued, unused leave beyond these 5 days will automatically expire on December 31st and will not be paid out.
